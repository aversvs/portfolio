## The project

Avante Nestlé is Nestlé's portal for healthcare professionals and students in Brazil. It offers free certified online courses, articles, videos, podcasts and events, all managed in Drupal.

I built it at F5 Web Design, together with the back-end and design teams.

## What I did

I created the portal's Drupal theme from scratch, starting from the design team's layouts.

- **Custom theme:** theme structure, Twig templates and styles, from the home page to the inner pages.
- **Blocks and Paragraphs:** content components that editors combine freely to build pages, without depending on developers.
- **jQuery interactions:** carousels, dropdown menus and dynamic interface components.
- **Back-end integration:** close collaboration to connect the front end to the CMS content and features.

![Online courses section, with a card for each course and its target audience](/projects/avante-nestle/corsi.webp)

## The result

The theme was created for the Brazilian portal and became a reusable base: today it is used on Nestlé sites in several countries.

Accessibility: **100/100 on mobile** and **97/100 on desktop** according to Lighthouse (measured in November 2026).

![Article page, with title, category, cover image and access to members-only content](/projects/avante-nestle/articolo.webp)

## What I learned

Building a theme meant to be reused in other countries taught me to think in independent, configurable components, the same approach I now apply in React.
